Evidence contract

Designed to fail closed—not fill the screen.

ProscanX treats loading, incomplete, failed, unavailable, and empty as different states. Conclusions and exports become available only when their evidence contract is satisfied.

01

Identity travels with the evidence

Assessment, backend, tenant, site, owner, target scope, and execution time remain attributable.

02

Coverage is part of the result

Completed, skipped, failed, and not-requested phases are visible rather than silently converted to zero.

03

Inference has a label

Observed topology, modeled attack paths, simulated outcomes, and formal claims remain distinguishable.

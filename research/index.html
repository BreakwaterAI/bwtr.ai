Research themes

Questions that cross product boundaries.

Our research themes reflect the systems Breakwater is building and the limits those systems must state honestly.

01

Networked and distributed systems

Asset identity, connectivity, protocol behavior, decentralized systems, and the evidence required to reason about infrastructure.

02

Adversarial systems

Attack paths, deception, mechanism design, and methods for challenging a security conclusion before an operator relies on it.

03

Cryptography and PQC transition

Observable cryptographic behavior, protocol interoperability, migration risk, and the difference between advertised and verified readiness.

04

AI/ML in consequential environments

How learning systems interact with clinical, operational, and security constraints where privacy, reliability, and human authority matter.

From research to product

How a research question becomes an operationally testable claim.

Research informs a product claim only after the evidence type, operating boundary, and failure conditions are explicit.

01

Frame

Define the security question and the decision it is meant to support.

02

Measure

Capture source, time, scope, coverage, and uncertainty.

03

Challenge

Replay, simulate, model, or formally analyze the claim using the method appropriate to it.

04

Operationalize

Carry validated evidence into a bounded product workflow without silently expanding authority.

Epistemic boundaries

Different evidence supports different claims.

Measured observations, simulations, formal results, literature-derived findings, and design targets are not interchangeable. Breakwater products preserve those distinctions so confidence does not outrun the method.

01

Measured

Observed in a defined environment at an attributable time and scope.

02

Modeled or simulated

Useful for reasoning and rehearsal, with assumptions and fidelity limits visible.

03

Formally established

Proved only within the stated model, properties, and assumptions.

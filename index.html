A system for defensible decisions

Evidence moves. Authority stays explicit.

Breakwater products share a disciplined operating loop designed for consequential security work.

01

Observe

Capture exact source, time, scope, and coverage before drawing a conclusion.

02

Validate

Replay, correlate, challenge, or simulate claims against independent evidence.

03

Decide

Prioritize by consequence and uncertainty, with assumptions visible.

04

Verify

Measure the result and preserve an attributable record of what changed.

About Breakwater

Built for decisions that need to hold up.

Breakwater is building a portfolio of evidence-led security products for software, connected infrastructure, and the post-quantum transition.

We believe a security conclusion should never travel without its source, scope, confidence, limits, and authority.

OUR MISSION

Make consequential security decisions more defensible—and safer to act on.

Security teams have more findings than they can defend, more tools than they can connect, and more pressure to automate. Breakwater focuses on the layer between observation and action: the evidence system that helps people understand what is known, what is inferred, and what they are authorized to do next.

That discipline applies whether the subject is application code, an operational network, a cryptographic migration, or a remediation plan.

How we build

Trust is an engineering property.

Our product principles are designed to survive contact with incomplete data, uncertain environments, and accountable operators.

01

Show the lineage

Important claims stay connected to their source, time, scope, and transformation.

02

State the boundary

Measured, inferred, simulated, and formally established results are not interchangeable.

03

Fail closed

Unavailable evidence is not translated into an empty inventory, zero exposure, or a clean result.

04

Keep people in authority

Automation may prepare and verify; consequential action remains governed and attributable.

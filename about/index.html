CO-FOUNDER & CEO

Nagu Thogiti

Nagu is a security researcher, computer scientist, and technology leader with more than two decades of experience spanning cybersecurity, AI/ML, distributed systems, cryptography, and protocol engineering.

Before founding Breakwater, he worked on protocol security and engineering at the Ethereum Foundation, including zero-knowledge systems and decentralized infrastructure. Earlier, at National Taiwan University Hospitals, he led multidisciplinary teams of engineers and physicians building AI healthcare systems deployed across Taiwan’s national healthcare system.

At Breakwater, Nagu leads company and product strategy, shaping how security evidence is turned into reliable models of assets, connectivity, attack paths, and operational risk.

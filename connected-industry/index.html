Connected industry

See cyber risk in production context.

PLCs, HMIs, industrial gateways, and the software dependencies behind them keep a production line running, often years past the point where every device can be patched. A finding that is routine on an office laptop can stop a line when it reaches a controller. Breakwater Secure, Assure, and SOAR give plant IT, OT engineering, and process safety teams one evidence trail from exposure to a change the line can absorb.

03GOVERN + VERIFY

Breakwater SOAR

Keep a production-system change inside maintenance-window and process-safety approval.

A configuration or segmentation change on a production system carries real safety and continuity stakes. SOAR opens the case with evidence attached, routes it to a named process-safety-aware approver, constrains execution to the agreed maintenance window, and re-tests the path after the change to confirm what actually closed.

  • Case evidence
  • Approval gates
  • Bounded execution
  • Outcome verification

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

A gateway added during a line expansion, closed inside a maintenance window.

An industrial gateway added during a line expansion turns up in a routine discovery pass, still reachable from the corporate network it was meant to be isolated from. Secure traces its firmware to a known weakness. A permitted reachability check confirms the exposed path.

SOAR opens a case, a named plant engineer approves a segmentation fix scheduled inside the next maintenance window, and Breakwater re-tests the path after the change ships. The record, what was found, what was proposed, who approved it, what was confirmed, stays attached to the gateway's file.

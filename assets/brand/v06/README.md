# Breakwater ASOC brand assets — v06

This directory contains the small, production-ready subset used by `bwtr.ai`.

- `corporate/`: the shared Breakwater header logo, without a tagline
- `products/`: the horizontal ASOC logo with its platform tagline, plus cleaner no-tagline
  Discover, Provenance and Response logos for pages that provide supporting copy
- `product-icons/`: compact ASOC, Discover, Provenance and Response app icons used in product navigation
- `icons/`: browser and Apple touch icons

Use the `light` SVGs on light surfaces and the `dark` SVGs on Vantablack surfaces. Do not recolor,
rewrite or optimize these vectors in place; their exact hashes are pinned in `brand-contract.json`.

The complete source kit, including every tagline variant, stacked layout, app icon, PNG export,
review boards and documentation, is preserved outside the public artifact at
`brand-archive/breakwater-logo-kit-v06/`.

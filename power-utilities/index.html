Electric power operations

Remote sites.
Long-lived systems.
Limited change windows.

Engineering workstations, supervisory systems, and remote access support the operation of distributed sites. Their security review needs the context of the equipment and the people who operate it.

Focus first on an agreed access boundary. Distinguish the evidence of connectivity from the authority to issue commands or change a protection setting.

An engineering-access review

Does the connection still match the job?

A contractor’s remote support work is complete. The engineering owner wants to know which systems remain reachable and whether the access still has an approved purpose.

The review combines the technical record with the work scope and site responsibility. Automatic revocation, protection testing, and live control changes are not assumed.

Where Breakwater fits

A reviewable model.
An engineering-owned decision.

Use asset and modeled-relationship evidence to frame the access question. The site team supplies operating purpose, equipment context, and authority for any change.

  • Security team

    Review the evidence, identify missing context, and separate candidates from confirmed conditions.

  • Engineering owner

    Determine the operational purpose and limits of the access.

  • Site or control-room team

    Coordinate any approved work through the existing operating process.

The supplied product media includes manufacturing examples, not a verified substation deployment. Power-specific capture and supported collection methods remain evaluation items. This page focuses on electric power; it does not imply blanket coverage of water, gas, or protection systems.

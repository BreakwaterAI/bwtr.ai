03GOVERN + VERIFY

Breakwater SOAR

Keep a substation change inside named engineering approval and rollback.

A segmentation or access change at a substation carries real safety and reliability stakes. SOAR opens the case with evidence attached, routes it to a named protection or OT engineer, constrains execution to the agreed scope, and re-tests the path after the change to confirm what actually closed.

  • Case evidence
  • Approval gates
  • Bounded execution
  • Outcome verification

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

A vendor's temporary access, closed with a named engineer's sign-off.

A remote-access gateway installed for a vendor's temporary maintenance window is still active six months later. Secure flags it against the utility's asset inventory. A permitted reachability check confirms it can reach a protection relay's engineering interface from outside the substation's intended boundary.

SOAR opens a case, a named protection engineer approves closing the access with a rollback plan, and Breakwater re-tests the path after the change. The record, what was found, what was proposed, who approved it, what was confirmed, stays attached to the substation's file.
